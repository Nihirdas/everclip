A clipboard that never forgets

Everything you copy, captured automatically and ready the moment you need it again.

Unlimited, deduplicated history

Text, rich text, links, images, screenshots and files — all captured the instant you copy. Copy the same thing twice and it just floats to the top instead of piling up.

Instant full-text search

An SQLite FTS5 index finds any snippet as you type, with filters by type and a Favorites tab for the things you paste all day.

Private by design

100% local. Password-manager copies (concealed & transient) are skipped automatically. Exclude any app, pause anytime, delete anything.

Paste back anywhere

Hit ⌥⌘V from any app, pick an item, and it pastes straight into whatever you were working in. Arrow keys and ⌘1–9 for the fastest picks.

Your clipboard, private and permanent — everything stays on your Mac.

No servers, no accounts, no telemetry. History lives in a local SQLite database under Application Support. Optional backup to your own Google Drive or Gmail is strictly opt-in and off by default.

Up and running in a minute

A tiny menu-bar app that stays out of your way until you need it.

01

Live in the menu bar

EverClip runs as a lightweight menu-bar agent — no Dock clutter. Enable launch-at-login and forget it's there.

02

Copy like normal

Keep working. Every copy is captured in the background in about a third of a second, with a thumbnail for images.

03

Recall with ⌥⌘V

Open the panel from anywhere, search or scroll, press Enter, and it pastes right back where you left off.
